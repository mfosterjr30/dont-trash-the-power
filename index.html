Why it matters

One wrong battery can put a whole collection system at risk.

Lithium-ion batteries can be crushed or damaged in garbage and recycling equipment, potentially causing fires. The right drop-off protects workers, facilities, and recoverable materials.

🔥

Prevent battery fires

Keep lithium-ion and other rechargeable batteries out of household trash and curbside recycling.

🛡️

Protect people

Safer handling reduces risks for sanitation workers, firefighters, recycling teams, and neighbors.

🌎

Recover resources

Specialized recycling can recover valuable materials and keep batteries in the right waste stream.

Start with three steps

Make the safer choice easy.

  • Identify the battery chemistry and type.
  • Protect terminals when required by the program.
  • Use a verified drop-off that accepts that exact battery.

Remember

Not every battery belongs in the same collection box.

Acceptance rules vary by battery chemistry, size, condition, and local program. Check before you go.
