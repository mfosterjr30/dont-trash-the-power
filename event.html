Battery Drive Day.

A community collection and education event concept designed to make responsible battery recycling visible, practical, and easy to understand.

The event concept

Bring batteries. Leave with better habits.

Proposed in partnership with a participating Home Depot store and an authorized collection/recycling partner. No store participation, sponsorship, or date has been confirmed.

  • Pre-approved list of accepted battery types.
  • Clearly marked collection stations and safety signage.
  • Trained staff or recycler representatives to manage collection.
  • Short demonstrations on identifying and preparing batteries.
  • Post-event reporting on participation and material collected.

Responsible event planning

Safety and approvals come first.

Before promoting or collecting anything, the organizers must secure written site approval, an authorized recycler, accepted-material rules, container specifications, trained handlers, and an emergency plan.

Confirm partners

Obtain written approval from the store and recycler. Confirm insurance, responsibilities, and logistics.

Publish acceptance rules

List exact chemistries, size/weight limits, condition restrictions, terminal protection, and excluded items.

Train & communicate

Train handlers, coordinate with local fire authorities as appropriate, and prepare signage and response procedures.
