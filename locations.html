Before you go: Check battery chemistry, size, weight, and condition; confirm the location accepts it; ask about terminal protection and packaging. Do not take damaged, defective, swollen, hot, leaking, or recalled batteries to ordinary collection boxes.
Local-first approach

Use your city or county rules.

Household battery programs can differ even between neighboring communities. Your local solid-waste authority or household hazardous waste program is the best source for local instructions.

A quick call can prevent a wasted trip.

Ask: “Do you accept this battery chemistry, size, and condition? Do I need to tape the terminals or bag each battery? Are there quantity limits?”
