Make the message move

Turn awareness
into community action.

Battery recycling gets easier when schools, workplaces, retailers, and neighborhood groups share consistent, accurate guidance.

🏫

Schools & youth groups

Host a battery safety lesson, create a “Battery Heroes” poster challenge, and share approved local drop-off resources with families.

🏢

Workplaces & businesses

Share safe storage guidance, identify approved collection options, and train staff not to place batteries in ordinary bins.

🤝

Neighborhood groups

Distribute multilingual information, invite local waste experts to speak, and promote existing authorized collection programs.

🧯

Fire & safety partners

Coordinate with local fire professionals on lithium-ion battery awareness and safe response messaging.

🛒

Retail partners

Explore an approved collection or education event only after the retailer and authorized recycler agree on requirements.

📣

Community champions

Share campaign materials, invite neighbors to check local rules, and direct people to verified drop-off resources.

A simple 30-day rollout

Start small. Build trust. Measure impact.

  • Week 1: Confirm local guidance and potential partners.
  • Week 2: Share safety content and recruit community champions.
  • Week 3: Run school, workplace, and neighborhood outreach.
  • Week 4: Evaluate engagement and plan the next activation.

Measure what matters.

Track participating organizations, event registrations, resource-page visits, verified drop-off referrals, volunteer participation, and—only through an authorized recycler—weight and types of batteries collected.
